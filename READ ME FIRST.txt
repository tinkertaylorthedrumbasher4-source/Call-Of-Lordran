BLACK OPS IN LORDRAN - what to send your friend (2026-10-25, build s236)
=====================================================================

This is the newest version, with everything so far:
- NEW: the guns break things - crates, barrels, pots, chairs, tables and the rest of what a sword
  can break, and Seath's Primordial Crystal. Explosions and the Thundergun break every one
  in reach. A teammate sees it break too.
- NEW: the ordinary undead are Kino der Toten's zombies - the Hollows of the Asylum, the Burg,
  the Depths, the Painted World and New Londo, and the Undead Soldiers - in the Kino uniforms
  with the four Kino zombie heads, still carrying their swords, torches, bows, spears and shields.
- NEW: Gwyn, Lord of Cinder is Five's Pentagon Thief (with Gwyn's sword, size and moves).
- NEW: the red dragon on the Undead Burg bridge is now Dead Ops Arcade's chicken - a giant one,
  about 13 m tall, that flies, flaps, lands and breathes fire like the dragon did.
- NEW: Double Points and Fire Sale power-ups. Kills now drop Max Ammo, Double Points or
  Fire Sale (one at a time). Double Points: every hit and kill pays double for 30 seconds.
  Fire Sale: every Mystery Box costs 10 points for 30 seconds, with the Fire Sale jingle.
  Each has its Black Ops model, glow, announcer, the character's own line and an icon at
  the bottom of the screen that blinks as it runs out. Whoever picks one up gives it to
  both players.
- Co-op revive: a player whose health runs out goes down for 45 seconds; walk up to him,
  face him and hold F for 3 seconds (1.5 with Quick Revive) to get him up. In co-op Quick
  Revive costs 1500 and doesn't revive you by itself - it makes you faster at reviving.
- Synced damage: hits on enemies count on both games.
- Each player's own Black Ops character shows on the other's screen, with the Black Ops
  animations and the gun in his hand; you hear each other's shots and character voice lines
  and see each other's bullet impacts and explosions.
- F9 menu: Noclip (on / off) - fly through walls: W/S where you look, A/D sideways, Space up,
  Ctrl or C down, Shift faster.
- F4: volume sliders for the mod's Black Ops sounds (master, weapons & effects, music, voices).
- Shared world: the Mystery Box opens and cycles on both screens (only the one who paid can
  take the gun), a Cymbal Monkey draws the enemies on both games, power-ups lie on both
  games, and a door or lever one player paid for is free for the other.

Both players need this same version. Older versions won't see each other in co-op.
